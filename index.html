Burger King Breakfast Hours

What Time Does Burger King Start and Stop Breakfast?

Most locations start around 6:00 AM and stop at 10:30 AM. This leaves time to buy a sandwich, a Croissan’wich or hash browns before the morning rush. If you plan to arrive close to 10:30 AM, check your local hours first.

Weekend, Holiday and 24-Hour Breakfast Hours

Many stores keep breakfast until 11:00 AM on weekends. On days like Christmas or Thanksgiving, hours are shorter. A 24-hour store also stops breakfast at 10:30 AM and then switches to the regular menu.

Why Breakfast Hours Vary by Location

Each owner sets local times. Airport, highway and Walmart locations often run different hours. The Burger King website or app shows the exact schedule for your restaurant.

Does Burger King Have All-Day Breakfast?

No. Burger King does not usually serve breakfast all day. Breakfast items are sold during set morning hours, and the menu changes to regular items afterward.

High-Protein and Lower-Calorie Choices

Egg and meat sandwiches give the most protein. The Ham, Egg & Cheese Croissan’wich has 18g of protein for 350 calories. Smaller portions and fewer extras keep calories lower.

Allergen Information

Biscuits, croissants, eggs and cheese can contain wheat, egg and milk. If you have a food allergy, read the official allergen list before ordering, because recipes can change.

Vegetarian and Vegan Options

Vegetarian choices include hash browns or a sandwich without meat, though eggs and dairy may still be present. Vegan choices are limited at breakfast. Ask the restaurant about ingredients and cooking methods before you order.

How to Order Burger King Breakfast Online

Ordering ahead saves time in the morning rush. The Burger King website and app show the menu, offers and pickup or delivery options for your nearest restaurant. You can also check the official Burger King website for current menus.

Can You Order Burger King Breakfast Online?

Yes, in most cases. Open the app or website and pick your nearest restaurant. Breakfast items show up only while that store serves breakfast. If you do not see them, breakfast time may be over.

Does Burger King Deliver Breakfast?

Many restaurants do, often through apps like DoorDash. Enter your address to see if a nearby store delivers breakfast. Fees and wait times change by area and time, so check them before you pay.

How to Order on the Burger King Mobile App

  1. Open the app and choose a nearby restaurant.
  2. Select your breakfast items.
  3. Choose pickup or delivery, if offered.
  4. Check coupons, review the order and pay.

Can You Get a Whopper During Breakfast Hours?

Not at every location. Some restaurants offer regular items in the morning, while others serve breakfast items only. Check the local menu or app before you go.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the healthiest option on the Burger King breakfast menu?

The Small Hash Browns are the lightest pick, with 230 calories. The Ham, Egg & Cheese Croissan’wich is another good choice, with 18g of protein.

Are Burger King hash browns gluten free?

No. Burger King hash browns in the USA are not considered gluten free, because they may contain wheat ingredients. Check the latest allergen information before ordering.

What are the most popular Burger King breakfast items?

Popular choices include the Sausage, Egg & Cheese Croissan’wich, the Bacon, Egg & Cheese Croissan’wich, French Toast Sticks and the Egg-Normous Burrito. Availability varies by location.

What is Burger King’s Ultimate Breakfast Platter?

The Ultimate Breakfast Platter is a filling meal with eggs, sausage and other breakfast sides. Availability varies by restaurant.

What are the most affordable Burger King breakfast items?

Small hash browns, French Toast Sticks and a small hot coffee are usually among the lower-priced picks. Prices vary by location, so check your local menu.

Does Burger King have breakfast combos?

Yes. A combo usually has a main item, hash browns and a small coffee or other drink. Combos and prices vary by restaurant.

Final Thoughts

The Burger King breakfast menu offers a simple answer to a busy morning: hot food that is quick to order and easy to eat. Croissan’wiches and burritos suit people who want a filling meal, while French Toast Sticks and pancakes fit a sweeter start. Hash browns and coffee complete most orders.

The right pick depends on time, taste and calorie goals. Lighter items work for people who count calories, and egg-and-meat sandwiches work for those who want more protein. Arrive before 10:30 AM on weekdays, check the BK app for deals, and confirm prices and hours with your local restaurant before you order.
